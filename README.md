# Ecommerce-Sales-Analytics-Dashboard-Power-BI
### Project Overview 
This project is an interactive Power BI dashboard designed to analyze year-to-date (YTD) ecommerce performance across multiple business dimensions. The goal is to provide business stakeholders with clear, actionable insights on sales, profit, customer segments, and product performance.

### Key Objectives
- Monitor overall business performance using YTD metrics.
- Identify top-performing and underperforming products.
- Compare regional and category-wise sales trends.
- Analyse customer purchasing behaviour across segments.
- Support data-driven decisions for marketing, supply chain, and inventory planning.
## Major Insights from the Dashboard
### Overall Performance
- YTD Sales: $11.53M, slightly down by 0.83%.
- YTD Profit: $1.34M, showing a healthy 4.50% increase.
- YTD Quantity Sold: 107.2K units, down by 7.29%, suggesting fewer transactions but possibly improved margins.
- Profit Margin: 11.58%, up by 5.37%, indicating efficient cost control.
### Category-Level Insights
| Category            | YTD Sales | YoY Change | Insight                                                        |
| ------------------- | --------- | ---------- | -------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Furniture**       | $2.52M    | **+0.73%** | Stable growth, consistent demand                               |
| **Office Supplies** | $6.92M    | **–1.22%** | Slight decline, may require pricing or promo adjustments       |
| **Technology**      | $2.10M    | **–1.37%** | Drop in performance, potential inventory or competition issues |

Top category remains Office Supplies, but it’s trending downward year-on-year.
### Product-Level Insights
- Staple Envelope, Staples, and Easy-staple Paper are the top 3 YTD revenue generators.
- Several products like Eldon Jumbo ProFile and Lexmark Printer show low YTD sales (<$0.40M)—candidates for review or discontinuation.
### Regional Performance
- West Region leads with $3.72M.
- Followed by East ($3.28M) and Central ($2.67M).
- South has the lowest sales at $1.87M.
- Strong sales in the West suggest better customer penetration or larger market share.
### Shipping Type Insights
- Standard Class accounts for the majority of sales (~60%).
- Second Class and First Class follow.
- Same-Day Shipping has the least share, which may indicate pricing sensitivity.
### Geographic Distribution
- The map visualization shows customer density and sales patterns by state.
- Regions like California, New York, and Texas appear among the strongest hotspots.
### Tools Used
- Power BI Desktop – for data modeling & visualization.
- DAX (Data Analysis Expressions) – calculated measures & KPIs.
- Excel/CSV Dataset – dataset preparation (uploaded by user).
- Power Query – data cleaning & ETL.

### Business Problems & Recommended Solutions (Data-Driven)
Below are the most critical business issues identified along with recommended actions.
#### Declining Sales & Quantity Sold
##### Problem
- Sales slightly down (–0.83%)
- Units sold down significantly (–7.29%)
##### Solutions
- Offer targeted discount campaigns for slow-moving products.
- Improve inventory forecasting to avoid stock-outs.
- Implement customer loyalty programs to increase repeat sales.
- Perform competitive price benchmarking.
#### Office Supplies & Technology Showing Negative YoY Growth
##### Problem
- Technology (–1.37%) and Office Supplies (–1.22%) declining YoY.
- These categories significantly influence total revenue.
##### Solutions
- Offer bundle promotions (e.g., laptop + accessories).
- Improve product visibility with SEO-optimized listings.
- Run category-level marketing campaigns.
- Analyze customer reviews to improve underperforming products.
#### South Region Underperforming
##### Problem
- South region shows low sales compared to others.
- Indicates poor regional penetration or supply chain delays.
##### Solutions
- Launch region-specific promotions.
- Improve delivery speed by reviewing logistics partners.
- Increase local digital marketing for regional customers.
#### High Sales but Low Profit Products
##### Problem
- Certain stock keeping unit generate revenue but minimal profit margin.
##### Solutions
- Negotiate better supplier terms.
- Reduce unnecessary discounting.
- Promote high-margin alternatives.
#### Customer Churn Risk
##### Problem
- Drop in quantity indicates possible loss of repeat customers.
##### Solutions
- Launch email remarketing campaigns.
- Offer free shipping thresholds.
- Introduce subscription models for frequently purchased products.
#### Summary of Business Recommendations
| Key Problem                | Strategic Solution                              |
| -------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------- |
| Declining sales & quantity | Customer retention + promotional campaigns      |
| Category decline           | Category marketing + supplier alignment         |
| Weak South region          | Geo-targeted marketing + shipping incentives    |
| Shipping imbalance         | Promote premium delivery options                |
| Underperforming products   | Discontinue, discount, or replace               |
| Stagnant sales growth      | Boost customer acquisition & expand offerings   |
| Uneven state performance   | Region-specific improvements                    |
| Category dependency        | Diversify catalog & invest in weaker categories |
